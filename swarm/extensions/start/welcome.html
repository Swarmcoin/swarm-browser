SWARM BROWSER

Welcome to SWARM Browser

A web browser with the SWARM wallet built in and privacy switches on from the first start. Everything below takes a minute to read.

Your wallet is in the toolbar

The SWARM Wallet button sits to the right of the address bar. Click it to create a wallet, restore one from its recovery phrase, or open yours. It works on SWARM mainnet.

Your keys stay on this computer, in a small wallet program that came with the browser; the browser itself never holds them.

The SWARM Navigator

The SWARM mark in the toolbar opens the Navigator on the side of the window: swarm.green, the Explorer, your wallet, the Messenger, node and mining, the Market and Support, in one place.

Both SWARM buttons are pinned when you start. If you unpin one, open the Extensions menu (the puzzle piece to the right of the address bar) and click the pin next to SWARM Start for the Navigator, or next to SWARM Wallet.

Privacy switches that are on

    Every new tab

    A new tab opens SWARM Start: a search box, SWARM shortcuts and a few tiles. One of them is the SWM price: while a SWARM Start tab is in view it asks the SWARM price service (wallet.swarm.green) once a minute, and nothing about you is sent. Switch “Show SWM price (USD)” off in its settings (the gear at the top right) and it asks nothing. The network figures are read only if you turn on “Network now” there.

    Searches go to the browser's default search engine.

    Where updates come from

    SWARM Browser does not update itself yet. A new version is a new download from swarm.green. SWARM Update checks once a day and tells you when one is out; it never downloads or installs anything by itself.
